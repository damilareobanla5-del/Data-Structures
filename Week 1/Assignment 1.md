1. Meaning of Queue
A queue is a data structure where the first item added is the first item removed.
It follows the FIFO principle, which means First In, First Out.
A good example is people waiting in a queue to buy something.

2. Operations of Queue
Enqueue – adds an item to the queue.
Dequeue – removes an item from the queue.
Peek – shows the first item.
isEmpty – checks if the queue is empty.
isFull – checks if the queue is full.
Display – shows all items in the queue.
